# CONTENT RULES — robstickler.com

This site is a public, indexed, permanent surface carrying Rob's name, and it is
an input to job applications. Anything on it should survive the same scrutiny as
a résumé line, because functionally it is one.

These rules exist because specific things went wrong before. Each one carries its
reason so a future editor doesn't "improve" it away.

This file and this repository are public. No private rationale, no medical
framing, no session lore belongs here. If a rule's full reasoning is not
public-safe, this file records the rule and its owner, not the reasoning.

The site serves at `robstickler.com`. The original host, `robstickler.vercel.app`,
still resolves and serves the same build; links already in the world stay valid.

---

## 1. Biographical claims come from the SSOT

Employment history, dates, titles, and role descriptions come from:

    Documents/Claude/Projects/Project Forge/job-search/resume/MASTER-resume-content.md

Dates and titles are frozen there. Don't paraphrase them from memory, don't
round tenure up, don't upgrade a title. If the site and MASTER disagree, MASTER
wins and the site gets corrected.

**Why:** one SSOT, or you get two Robs with different résumés.

## 2. Only Pizza Hut carries performance numbers

The complete list of sanctioned performance metrics for Rob's employment history:

- 40% year-over-year sales growth
- ~30-person team
- Manager of the Year, Western Washington, 1998

That's it. No other role gets a number attached to Rob's performance.

**Why:** these three are documented and defensible. Nothing else in the
employment history has a verifiable figure behind it, and an unverifiable number
is worse than no number — it invites exactly the scrutiny it can't survive.

## 3. No studio figures at all — ruled 2026-08-28, superseding 2026-08-16

No figure from any studio's books appears on this site: no membership counts,
retention or churn numbers, lifetime values, revenue figures, ceilings or
floors, whatever their provenance. The earlier allowance for "market or trove"
figures on /studios is superseded; the ledger figures that page carried were
removed by Rob's ruling on 2026-08-28. Never reintroduce them.

**The activity is still fine, numberless.** "Ran the entire operation," "built
the reporting pipeline," "produced a retention analysis for ownership" — all
true, all sanctioned. /studios now argues from the prospect's own books (the
three-questions section), which is stronger than publishing anyone's history.

## 4. Never name the successor entity

The wellness studio Rob operated is referred to generically, or by its own name
where his employment history requires it. The business that acquired its assets
is never named on this site. Entity-boundary discipline is not optional.

If you need to know which entity this refers to, ask Rob or Paul. It is
deliberately not written here, because this file is public.

## 5. Don't claim what Rob hasn't done

Specifically and permanently: **no Lean/Six Sigma, no MRP/ERP, no manufacturing
experience.** He has none of them. Claiming a preferred qualification he lacks
is the fastest available way to lose an interview he would have gotten.

## 6. Every number traces to MASTER §3 or §4

Before any figure goes on the page, find it in MASTER §3 (experience) or §4
(projects). If it isn't there, it doesn't ship. §4 carries per-project
verification guards — read them. Known ones:

- **BestieScorecard** — verified-safe: 158 episodes, ~2,000 forecasts
  *extracted* (2,031), **368 scored**. Never write "2,000 scored" — extracted
  and scored are different numbers. Keep calibration findings qualitative.
- **PulseResume** — "197 automated tests" is verified and safe.
- **AI Knowledge Manual** — no page count, no "fully paged," no
  "zero-broken-link" claim. The paging pass was lossy.

## 7. Studio tenure is one continuous span — ruled 2026-08-27

Sep 2022 to May 2026, about three and a half years, continuous. The studio
changed names in 2024; same entity, same job, one span. The site says so
plainly ("It opened under an earlier name"). Never "twenty months" — a stale
figure that sat on this site for months and propagated into downstream work.
Never "four years."

## 8. The site is ops-first — ruled 2026-08-28

Identity order on the hire path: people and P&L first, systems second. The
homepage speaks as an operator; the systems line ("I don't write the code...")
opens /proof, it does not lead the site. Proof chips on the homepage carry
sanctioned numbers only (rules 2 and 6).

Nav order is recruiter-first: Work, Proof, How I operate, Studios, Interests,
Photographs. /proof was renamed from /artifacts on 2026-08-28 with a permanent
redirect; the old URL keeps working.

**Proof-page bar:** a card must be openable or verifiable quickly, and it must
be hire evidence, not lab notebook. Run-count trophies (largest fan-out, idea
tallies) stay off the page. Hobby apps live on /interests.

## 9. How I operate is deliberately public

A working-style guide, on purpose: clarity, an explicit check-for-understanding
step, and the Pizza Hut training model (show and tell, do it with me, watch me
do it). It is framed as a standard held, not a limitation disclosed. Keep it
that way.

## 10. Disclosure is per surface — never harmonize

The footer colophon describes how THIS SITE was made: specified, directed, and
corrected by Rob; prose AI-crafted at his direction; claims his and verified.
Rob's essays carry a different, stricter note. The two are different because
the surfaces were made differently, and both are true. Merging them into one
sentence would make it false somewhere. /method is the disclosure's long-form
home; it stays unlinked and noindexed until Rob rules it live, and its story
section is Rob's own typed sentences or nothing.

## 11. Voice

No em dashes in site copy. Nothing that reads as AI-written. Plain sentences,
concrete claims, honest hedges only where the fact itself is hedged.

## 14. Verify links before printing them

Any external link gets a live check before it ships — and check the response
body, not just the status code. A 200 from an SPA shell is a false positive;
a stale "dead link" hold suppresses a working asset. Both failure modes have
happened.

the verification guards. Guessing produces a claim nobody can defend later.
There is no deadline here worth that.
