# CONTENT RULES — robstickler.vercel.app

This site is a public, indexed, permanent surface carrying Rob's name, and it is
an input to job applications. Anything on it should survive the same scrutiny as
a résumé line, because functionally it is one.

These rules exist because specific things went wrong before. Each one carries its
reason so a future editor doesn't "improve" it away.

---

## 1. Biographical claims come from the SSOT

Employment history, dates, titles, and role descriptions come from:

    Documents/Claude/Projects/Project Forge/job-search/resume/MASTER-resume-content.md

Dates and titles are frozen there. Don't paraphrase them from memory, don't
round tenure up, don't upgrade a title. If the site and MASTER disagree, MASTER
wins and the site gets corrected.

**Why:** one SSOT, or you get two Robs with different résumés.

## 2. Only Pizza Hut carries performance numbers

The complete list of sanctioned performance metrics for Rob's employment history:

- 40% year-over-year sales growth
- ~30-person team
- Manager of the Year, Western Washington, 1998

That's it. No other role gets a number attached to Rob's performance.

**Why:** these three are documented and defensible. Nothing else in the
employment history has a verifiable figure behind it, and an unverifiable number
is worse than no number — it invites exactly the scrutiny it can't survive.

## 3. No studio performance metrics — but read the distinction

**Never on the site:** client counts, retention or churn figures, review counts,
campaign sizes, membership numbers — anything measuring *how Rob performed* at
the wellness studio. This rule is permanent, affirmed 2026-07-21, and it is not
subject to a good argument.

**The activity is fine.** "Ran the entire operation," "built the site and SEO,"
"built the reporting pipeline," "diagnosed and fixed email deliverability" — all
true, all sanctioned, all numberless.

**The distinction that matters, and it cuts the other way:**

A *market or opportunity figure* — sizing a problem in the industry, drawn from
books Rob owns — is a different animal from a *performance claim about Rob's own
work*. The first is analysis. The second is a résumé assertion. Rob ruled on
2026-08-16 that the /studios figures are his own trove and clear to use.

**Do not over-correct.** Stripping every number near the word "studio" would
gut a legitimate consulting argument. Ask: *is this measuring Rob, or measuring
the market?* Measuring Rob → off-paper. Measuring the market → allowed, and
Rob has ruled.

## 4. Never name the successor entity

The wellness studio Rob operated is referred to generically — "a wellness
studio," or by its own name where his employment history requires it. The
business that acquired its assets is never named on this site. It is a separate
business with its own owner, and entity-boundary discipline is not optional.

If you need to know which entity this refers to, ask Rob or Paul. It is
deliberately not written here, because this file is public.

## 5. Don't claim what Rob hasn't done

Specifically and permanently: **no Lean/Six Sigma, no MRP/ERP, no manufacturing
experience.** He has none of them.

This comes up because they appear as "preferred qualifications" on roles he is
otherwise genuinely qualified for. Claiming a preferred qualification he lacks is
the fastest available way to lose an interview he would have gotten.

## 6. Every number traces to MASTER §3 or §4

Before any figure goes on the page, find it in MASTER §3 (experience) or §4
(projects). If it isn't there, it doesn't ship.

**§4 carries per-project verification guards. Read them — they exist because
these specific numbers have drifted before.** Known ones:

- **BestieScorecard** — verified-safe: 158 episodes, ~2,000 forecasts
  *extracted* (2,031), **368 scored** (117 correct / 150 partial / 101 wrong).
  Never write "2,000 scored" — extracted and scored are different numbers and
  conflating them is the failure mode. Keep calibration findings **qualitative**;
  no domain hit-rates, because n is low and those numbers describe the podcast
  hosts, not Rob.
- **PulseResume** — "197 automated tests" is verified and safe.
- **AI Knowledge Manual** — do **not** claim a specific page count, "fully
  paged," or a "zero-broken-link" sweep. The paging pass was lossy.

## 7. Verify links before printing them

Any external link gets a live check before it ships. A broken portfolio link on
a personal brand site is a visible bug, and it's the kind a hiring manager
notices.

As of 2026-08-16, verified live: `pulseresume.netlify.app`,
`github.com/rdeanstickler-star/pulseresume`, `bestiescorecard.com`.

**Note the failure mode in both directions:** those PulseResume links sat gated
as "dead/unconfirmed" for roughly five weeks *after* they were working. A stale
hold suppressed a strong asset. Re-check before you assume either way.
